    Objections

    A.I.R.

    AAgree or acknowledge. Show them you heard it. Never argue with the first no.
    I.R.Immediately return. Bring it back to why you are there, then keep going.
    The longer answer

    Feel, Felt, Found

    "I understand how you feel. Others felt the same. Here is what they found." About 80 percent empathy, 20 percent solution. It sits next to A.I.R. on the objection board.

    The three levers

    Pace, Pitch, Attitude

    Most slumps start with one of three. The app reads the funnel and tells the rep which one, so they stop guessing.

    For reps

    • One tap countersTap the furthest step reached and every earlier step counts too. Undo reverses a tap, and minus fixes a single box.
    • One fix searchType a struggle that keeps coming back, like "they keep saying not interested." Get the one thing to try next, plus a quick Yes or Not yet self check that rechecks after ten more conversations.
    • Pace, Pitch, Attitude readThe app reads the funnel and tells the rep which of the three levers is costing them sales.
    • Objection prepA.I.R. and Feel Felt Found answers to practice before the next conversation, not scripts to read in the middle of one. Retail teams also get 15 and 5 and Turn and Return.
    • Training boardShort, searchable lessons: the ten step sale flow, know your product, and ten work habits like Be Prepared, Know Your Why and Focus on Controllables.
    • SMART goals and goal helperReps start from what they no longer want, turn it into a 30, 60 or 90 day goal, and watch each SMART letter light up as the goal gets sharper.
    • Income paceEnter an income target and see the daily conversations, presentations and asks needed to hit it, and how improving one step cuts the work required.
    • Daily routine check insReps plan their day around their goals and check off what they actually did.
    • Callback alertsSave a return address or pinned spot and a time. Alerts arrive ten minutes before and right on time, even with the phone locked.
    • Leave by warningOptional. Estimates walking time back to a callback so reps leave in time. It is not location tracking for managers.
    • FollowupsLog a promise with a due time and how to reach them: go back, call or text, with a name and number.
    • Speed test loggingFiber reps log observed speeds at the door, with daily counts and averages in reports.
    • Time off requestsReps enter a day or a range, and see Seen by your manager when it is acknowledged.
    • Works anywhereOffline taps sync when signal returns. No app store, any phone, saved to the home screen. High contrast mode for bright sun.

    Questions

    What owners ask before they start.

    My reps will not log. Why would this be different?

    It asks for one tap, not a form. About ten seconds after a conversation, and the rep gets something back right away: their own one fix. You also see who is logging, so it becomes part of the day.

    We already have a CRM or a map app. Do we need this?

    Those track addresses, pins and leads. Furthest Step tracks how each conversation went and coaches the rep on it. Keep your map app for where to go, and use this for what happens at the door.

    What happens in the 14 days?

    David sets up your team with your objections and standards. Your reps open one link and start logging. You watch the board for two weeks. Nothing is charged during the 14 days.

    What if there is no signal at the door?

    Taps are saved on the phone and sync on their own when the signal comes back. Saved taps sync when the connection returns.

    Who sees the numbers?

    Reps see their own numbers and coaching. Managers see their teams. Owners control every key and can remove access at any time.

    The intro price ends and your plan moves to its full price: $299 a month for Crew, or $699 a month for Office. That is the whole price, shown here up front. No long term contract.
