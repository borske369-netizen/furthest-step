How it works

One tap after every conversation.

The rep taps the furthest step the conversation reached. Every earlier step counts too. About ten seconds, even with no signal.

  • DDecision maker. They reached the person who can say yes.
  • PPresentation. They gave the pitch.
  • CClose. They asked for the business. Close does not mean they got the deal.
  • SSale. They said yes.
How to read it: fix the step right before the drop.
  • Talking to decision makers, but not getting to present? Fix the opener.
  • Presenting, but not asking for the business? Fix the presentation.
  • Asking for the business, but not getting the sale? Fix how you ask.

Where the app really shines

Reps see where they are breaking down, and correct it themselves.

Right on their phone, each rep sees where today is breaking down. In the example, Tasha asked for the business on only 4 of 11 presentations. Presenting, but not asking for the business, so her fix is the presentation. Nobody had to tell her.

  • Each bar is the share that made it from the step before
  • When a bar is low, it tells her which step to fix
  • This week so far: how often she asks for the business, how often asking turns into a sale, and decision makers per sale
  • Her own numbers, not a team average

The game board

Treat sales like a game. Stay in bounds.

Everyone loses their attitude from time to time. The board keeps reps focused on the right things. It is the field, and everything on it is in the rep's control: Pace, Pitch and Attitude, each habit with a plain explanation. When a rep focuses on what they control, they stay in bounds. When they focus on what they cannot control, like the turf or the weather, they are out of bounds, and their attitude goes with it.

Every item on the field

Pace, Pitch and Attitude, with habits like Set Your Door Goal, Get Every Decision Maker in the Conversation and Assume The Sale. Each one says what to do and why it works.

In bounds: search it

Same problem keeps happening? Type it. "Too expensive" is an objection, so it is in bounds. The board gives the A.I.R. and Feel, Felt, Found response, ties it to the rep's own numbers, and asks: are you closing with confidence? Yes or Not yet.

Out of bounds: it counts

"My manager gave me a bad turf" is out of bounds. The app shows the chain: focus goes to something you cannot move, attitude drops, pace drops, and the next customer gets the worst version of you. It counts how many times today, then puts them right back on the board.

Out of bounds is a focus reminder for the rep, not a write up. The search matches what they type against the board, so the answers are the same trusted plays every time.

Callbacks and follow ups

Told to come back at 6? It makes sure they do.

For door to door teams, a callback is a sale waiting to happen. Reps pin the spot, set the time and add a name and number in a few taps.

  • An alert 10 minutes before and at the time, even with the app closed
  • A leave by alert based on how far away they are
  • A warning when they are too far to make it on time
  • One tap fills in an on my way text to the homeowner
  • An opener ready to go: "I said I would come back, and I wanted to keep my word"
  • Follow ups for anything else they promised, by visit, call or text
  • Location stays on the rep's phone

Goals, pace and routine

As deep as each rep wants to go.

Some reps just tap and go. Others want to build a plan. The goals and routine pages are there for every rep who wants to grow.

SMART goals

A helper starts with "What do you not want anymore?" As they type a 30, 60 or 90 day goal, each SMART letter lights up when the goal gets sharper.

Their pace, in plain math

$5,000 a month at $100 a sale is 50 sales a month. From their own numbers, that is 28 decision makers a day, and it shows how improving one step cuts the work.

A routine that is scored

Type or photograph a routine and it becomes a morning and evening checklist, scored on whether it actually supports their goals, with what to add next.

Questions

What owners ask before they start.

My reps will not log. Why would this be different?

It asks for one tap, not a form. About ten seconds after a conversation, and the rep gets something back right away: where their own day is breaking down. You also see who is logging, so it becomes part of the day.

We already have a CRM or a map app. Do we need this?

Those track addresses, pins and leads. Furthest Step tracks how each conversation went and coaches the rep on it. Keep your map app for where to go, and use this for what happens at the door.

What happens in the 14 days?

Furthest Step support sets up your team with your objections and standards. Your reps open one link and start logging. You watch the board for two weeks. Nothing is charged during the 14 days.

What if there is no signal at the door?

Taps are saved on the phone and sync on their own when the signal comes back. Saved taps sync when the connection returns.

Is it only for door to door?

No. Every sale has steps, a close and objections. Furthest Step support sets it up for how your team sells, with your steps, objections, talk tracks and standards. A retail floor version already exists.

Does the game board search use AI?

No. It matches what the rep types against the plays on the board, so the answer is the same trusted response every time.

Who sees the numbers?

Reps see their own numbers and coaching. Managers see their teams. Owners control every key and can remove access at any time.
