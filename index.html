    Straight answers

    What owners ask before they start.

    My reps will not use another app. Why would this be different?

    Because it asks for one tap, not a form. It takes about ten seconds after a conversation, and reps get something back right away: their own one fix. Managers also see who is logging, so it becomes part of the day fast.

    We already use a CRM or a door knocking app. Do we need this?

    Most of those track addresses and leads. Furthest Step tracks how each conversation went and coaches the rep on it. Many teams run both: the CRM for the lead, Furthest Step for the rep.

    Is $333 a month a lot for a small office?

    It is about three sales a month for the whole team at a $150 sale. If the coaching helps each rep close just one more a month, it has paid for itself several times over. And you get 14 days to see it with your own numbers first.

    How fast can my team start?

    Most teams are live the same day. You get a team code, your reps open a link on their phone, and they are tapping on the next door. We load your industry steps and training during setup.

    Do reps need to download an app?

    No. It runs in the phone's browser and can be saved to the home screen like an app. iPhone and Android both work.

    What if there is no signal in the field?

    Taps are saved on the phone and sync automatically when the signal comes back. Nothing gets lost.

    Can it match our scripts and our offer?

    Yes. Your steps, your standards, your objection answers and your training go on the board. That is part of every plan.

    Who can see the numbers?

    Reps see their own numbers and coaching. Managers see their teams. Owners control every key and can remove access at any time.

    What happens after the free 14 days?

    Your plan starts on day 15. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. No contracts after that either; you can cancel any month.
